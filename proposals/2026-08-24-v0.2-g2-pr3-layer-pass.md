# 2026-08-24 — v0.2 — the G2 pass over PR3's layer

`Status:` Decided → [2026-08-24 — the PR3-layer freeze](../decisions/2026-08-24-pr3-layer-freeze-charter.md)
`Target:` v0.2 — one dedicated slice, sequenced **before PR8**
`Filed:` 2026-08-24
`Review:` Unreviewed

The slice this proposal plans is the "revisit at G2" the 2026-08-20 ruling promised,
brought forward by `decisions/2026-08-24-pr3-layer-freeze-charter.md` (draft at
filing). One deliberate, fully-reviewed pass over `src/topology/**`, the enforcement
layer that ranges over it, and the test infrastructure that observes it — with the
owner's stated bar: **the best code and test coverage this project can produce,
effort explicitly not a constraint.** The constraint that remains is the one the
layer's record demands: every behaviour change reviewed, measured, and separated from
mechanical churn, because this layer's failure mode has never been effort — it is the
silent weakening (PR5 round 7) and the repair-introduced defect (three in PR7 alone).

**Why now, in one sentence:** PR8's merge queue builds directly on lease semantics
that are measurably wrong-by-omission today, and every workstream below gets more
expensive after another slice lands on top.

## Scope

- `src/topology/**` — the vocabulary: fold, effects, events, registry, leases,
  census, paths, schema, queue.
- `DESIGN.md:222` — **not touched by this pass.** An earlier revision listed it as
  reachable via a Class-C decision at W4; that decision is withdrawn and the line is
  unchanged.
- `src/effects.rs` — the classifier and census machinery (`production_region`,
  `production_code`, `externally_reachable_fns`).
- Test scaffold (`FoldedEmitter`, the shared hook bundle) and the withheld
  catalogues.
- Master, **already used** for W10's standards document and its mechanical gates:
  `CODING_STANDARDS.md` landed by its own pull request and this pass opens no second
  one. Listed because the scope statement is historical, not because work remains.

Non-goals: no new driver features, no PR8 work smuggled in, no packet amendments
beyond the errata the owner authorises in writing first.

## Workstreams

Ordered. W1–W3 are behaviour (Class B/C, each with its mutation witness in the same
commit); **W4 is empty** — its question closed without a change, so it carries no behaviour
and owes no witness; W5–W9 are structure and coverage. **W10 is not one workstream and not all
future work:** W10.1 (the standards document) **already landed** and is history;
W10.2, the layer's cleanliness sweep, is mechanical and runs last in this pass; W10.3,
the whole-tree sweep, is a **separate slice after** this pass and is not pulled into
it. Reading "W10 runs last" as one undivided step either duplicates landed work or
drags the whole-tree sweep into this slice.

**W0 — pre-pass, may land on `slice/pr7` before it merges.** Fix the two rustdoc
attachment defects around `questions_open` / `predicted_region` /
`frozen_rung_binding`; refresh `PR7-FOLD-ACCESSORS-IN-PR3-LAYER` to the tree's real
numbers; add the §3 adjudication row and the §2 region-validation row per the
charter.

**W1 — the fold refuses a divergent recorded region.** `check_dispatched` today
validates a `task_dispatched` lease's shape only, and `apply_dispatched` grants
whatever region the event carries — while `check_attempt_started` refuses a binding
the fold did not derive (`FoldError::BindingMismatch`). Close the asymmetry: an
ordinary dispatch whose `Predicted { paths }` differs from `fold::predicted_region`'s
answer is refused by name. This converts the class of PR7's one-commit defect from
convention-guarded to structurally impossible — the eleventh reader's own commit
message states the mechanism: *"it would fail LOUD rather than silently, because the
fold refuses rather than accepts."* Needs the owner's packet erratum first (see
"Owner inputs"): the recorded region's contract becomes *derivation-checked*, same as
the binding's, which also pins that the derivation may not change across versions
without a schema bump. Tests: a hint-shape grid varied one axis at a time — literal,
mid-glob, leading `**/`, `?` `[` `{`, backslashes, trailing separators, empty list,
empty-literal — each asserting the refusal names the divergence, plus the round-trip
that the fold's own answer is accepted. The PR7 regression assertion stays as the
driver-side witness.

**W2 — complete `matches_override`, collapse the half-rule reader.**
`matches_override` compares agent, model and effort and ignores `tier` and `pinned`;
`frozen_rung_binding` is *"deliberately only half of the fold's rule"* because of it.
Complete the validator (all five fields, each perturbation refused under its own
name), then make the reader the whole rule — one derivation, one authority, no
unreachable-arm apology. This unblocks PR9's answer-ingest branch from inheriting a
known-partial check.

**W3 — the effects.rs errata the ruling deferred.** Three Class-C changes, each
pre-authorised by the charter, each with its packet erratum written by the owner
first: the probe execution context (`PR4-SPAWN-SITE-PROBE-CONTEXT` — the site
variant, its adjacency and fault row, so ST-07 evidence finally covers the probe
prefixes); the `Ref.CreateIntegration` order axis (`PR7-CREATEINTEGRATION-ORDER-
BACKWARDS` — one token, plus the two transcription tests, plus a G2-evidence check
that nothing was measured against the wrong order); the three `Answer.*` module
columns (`PR5-ANSWER-MODULE-COLUMN` — regenerate `effect_sites.json`, retire the
`funnel-modules.json` disagreement note). Re-run every census the registry feeds.

**W4 — `CommandSpec.program`: the question is closed, and this workstream is empty.**
`PR4-PROGRAM-PATH-NOT-UNICODE` asked whether a non-Unicode agent path is representable or
refused at the boundary as today. **It is closed as not reproducible in production**, by
`decisions/2026-08-25-commandspec-program-stays-string.md`.

The premise was that a resolved agent-binary path can reach `CommandSpec.program` and be
refused there. It cannot. `Invocation::at`, the constructor that takes a path, is
`#[cfg(test)]` and says so in its own doc; production's only constructor takes a **bare CLI
name**, which is valid Unicode by construction. `runner/mod.rs` states that a `String` was
always wide enough for that name, and `runner/host.rs` splits `PATH` as an `OsStr` and joins
the name to each entry, so a CLI under a non-UTF-8 directory is found and executed today
without the field ever holding a path.

**What W4 therefore does not do**, each item withdrawn from an earlier revision of this
workstream:

1. it does **not** widen `CommandSpec.program`;
2. it does **not** make a `DESIGN.md:222` edit — the line is unchanged and no decision
   schedules one;
3. it does **not** replace `a_program_path_a_string_cannot_carry_is_refused_by_name`. **That
   test stays.** It exercises a state production cannot construct, which is what a defensive
   refusal is for: if a path-valued constructor is ever added, the boundary fails closed
   instead of spawning a lossily converted path that names something else. Deleting it would
   remove the guard that makes adding such a constructor safe;
4. it does **not** carry the deletion of `CODING_STANDARDS.md` §1's "Known conflicts at
   adoption" block. That block retires on **its own motion**, by its own pull request to
   `master`, and not as cargo on a spec edit that is not happening.

   **Its status changed with the withdrawal, and the change is worth stating.** An owner
   ruling of 2026-08-28 rejected a finding that called §1 inaccurate, accepting the narrower
   reading that it was *incomplete*: at that time the conflict genuinely was unresolved and
   W4 genuinely was its venue. Neither holds now — the question is closed, the venue decided
   nothing, and the two passages never disagreed once the field is read as a name rather than
   a path. **§1 is therefore inaccurate now, where it was only incomplete then.** That is a supersession by later evidence, not a reversal of the ruling, and it
   makes the retirement a correctness repair rather than tidying.

**W4 is retained as a numbered workstream with nothing in it**, rather than removed, so the
sequencing and the exit criteria below keep their references and a reader of either finds
the closure rather than a gap.

**W5 — design the fold's read surface once, and give the layer its structure.**
Twelve readers accreted in one slice is the smell objection 6 of the challenge
named — eleven at the challenge, and `open_no_attempt` at `ffcc74a` after it,
under its own Class A row.
Survey the reader call sites — **36 measured at `3c09f6e`, and the tree has added
readers and consumers since, so W5 re-measures at its own head rather than working
from that number** — plus PR8/PR9's needs from the packet, and decide
the driver's *entitlement API* as a set: what the loop may ask, what it may never
re-derive, naming, poison-guard policy stated on the type rather than per-function
folklore. Collapse, rename, or regroup all **twelve** into that design.
`#[warn(missing_docs)]` (or the standards' equivalent) on the module so the
attachment class fails loud.

The same workstream owns **structural decomposition**: `fold.rs` (~470 KB),
`effects.rs` (~488 KB), `events.rs` (~244 KB) and `census.rs` (~155 KB) are single
files; split them along the seams the standards name (one concept per module),
proved behaviour-neutral by the identity techniques W10.2 uses. Two hard edges the
decomposition must not cross without a Class-C decision: **the wire** — serde-visible
names, tags and payload shapes in `events.rs` are design content, recorded runs
replay through them, and the wire-pinning tests are the tripwire — and **the
packet-transcribed inventories**, whose text moves only on an owner erratum. Module
moves regenerate every checked-in artifact and re-run every census whose expected
counts name a file.

**W8 — re-measure the withheld catalogue.** This workstream is **re-measurement, not
application**, and the number it was written against is superseded.
`reviews/2026-08-25-pr7-g2-evidence.md` records that application follows the

because the driver they were authored against is PR7's. The evidence says in its own

So W8 does **not** re-apply a 93-entry cut; doing so would repeat work already done
and omit the 22 entries the larger cut adds. It re-measures the previously-killed set
on the post-W1–W7 tree, because this pass is itself "two production-changing rounds
after the last measurement", and triages survivors by the standing rules (repair, row,
or hardening). Exit criterion 3 is claimed against that re-measurement.

**W9 — the registry stops grading its own homework.** `the_observable_orders_are_
the_ones_the_adjacency_admits` asserts two functions agree and is green for either

**W10 — standards, then cleanliness, in that order and last.**
1. **Already landed — this item is history, not work.** `CODING_STANDARDS.md` is on
   master with its mechanical gates, by its own pull request as this item required.
   It absorbed the conventions that were enforced but scattered: the CLAUDE.md hard
   rules (panic policy, error taxonomy, `std::path`, edition/MSRV), plus what this
   branch's record earned — doc-comment attachment discipline, `#[cfg(test)]`
   placement (items after production, the truncation trap), census and blanker rules
   (strip-and-assert, positive controls, structure over substring), fixture
   independence (vary every meaningful field, distinct-value counts), test naming as
   claims, one-implementation-per-rule. **Do not create a second normative document,
   and do not add a gate for what is already gated.** The rule that everything
   machine-checkable gets a gate — `clippy.toml` extension, `[lints]`, rustfmt config,
   or a bash gate — **was satisfied when this landed**: four bash gates exist, which
   `CLAUDE.md` and `.github/scripts/` both say, and the "seven" this item was drafted
   against is a pre-merge count. It binds only a *future* rule that arrives without a
   mechanism, not the pass. The standards text marks each rule *enforced* or
   *reviewed*, so a cleanliness finding can quote its passage the way a packet finding
   does.
2. The cleanliness sweep of the layer then runs as the pass's final commits:
   mechanical, behaviour-neutral by construction, proved neutral per file (sorted
   line-multiset identity where a change is pure movement; the full suite plus
   re-measured mutation samples where it is not), and reviewed under the standards'
   text only. No behaviour edit rides in a cleanliness commit; no cleanliness edit
   rides in a behaviour commit. The separation is what makes both reviewable.
3. **The rest of the tree gets its own sweep slice, after this pass and before
   #18's merge candidate.** The integration branch supersedes master when #18
   merges, so all code cleanup happens **on the integration branch** — code
   cleaned on master directly is merge friction with every in-flight slice, and
   the work reaches master anyway, once and attested. Only the standards
   document itself lands on master directly. The full order is therefore:
   PR7 → this pass (behaviour + layer sweep) → the whole-tree sweep slice
   (per-module commits, same neutrality proofs, legacy engine included) → #18.
   An "on-touch only" migration for legacy code is rejected: it would ship
   v0.2.0 with code never brought under the standard.
4. **Prior standards-oriented review output is salvaged by file, not wholesale.**
   A pre-existing review of either tree feeds the sweep work-lists where the
   reviewed file is **byte-identical** since that review's SHA — verify by hash,
   never by path or line count — and is re-derived where it is not. Staleness is
   asymmetric: master-origin files have barely moved and their findings mostly
   carry (onto the integration branch, where the cleanup happens); findings
   against files the slices changed are re-derived by the work-list review this
   workstream already runs.

## Sequencing and ceremony

- One slice, one pull request into `codex/parallelism-design`
  (`decisions/2026-08-21-stacked-slice-prs.md`), after `slice/pr7` merges. W10's
  standards pull request **has already targeted master and merged**, so nothing in
  this sequence waits on it. The whole-tree sweep (W10.3) is
  deliberately **not** part of this slice: it follows as its own pull request
  into the integration branch, after this pass and before #18's merge candidate.
- Behaviour before structure before churn, with coverage split by
  anchor-sensitivity (owner decision, 2026-08-26):
  **W1–W4 → W7 → W5–W6 → W8–W9 → W10.2.** Findings first, because tests
  written before behaviour fixes pin the wrong behaviour. W7 (the emitter
  routing) moves ahead of the structural work because it strengthens the
  detection net every later phase runs under. W8–W9 (catalogue **re-measurement**
  — see W8; application already happened as PR7's evidence
  and the censuses) run after the structure settles, because their anchors
  are names, paths and per-file counts — measured before the decomposition
  they would all be re-measured after it. The sweep stays last, under the
  maximal net. The governing principle: each phase's evidence must be valid
  for the tree that ships. Any workstream that slips does not hold the
  others hostage — each lands reviewable on its own, with its ledger rows
  updated in the same commit (§4's mechanical rule).
- Every Class-B/C change carries its mutation witness and names the packet passage
  or erratum it satisfies. Reviews read this proposal, FINDINGS.md, and the charter
  as inputs; the frontier review of the merge candidate per MAINTAINING.md.
- The suite runs on all three CI platforms; W3 and W6 additionally re-run the
  affected census artifacts and check them in.

## Exit criteria

1. Every FINDINGS §2 row owned by *"the post-v0.2 pass over PR3's layer"* or by
   *"PR6/PR7 implementer"* against a frozen file is repaired here or explicitly
   re-dispositioned by the owner with a dated reason.
2. The accepted deviation of 2026-08-20 that W3 owns is resolved, and the region contract
   is validated in the fold (W1). W4's deviation is resolved by closure rather than by a
   change: its finding is not reproducible in production.
3. The withheld catalogue is **re-measured at this pass's own head** and reports zero
   unexplained survivors. Not "zero unapplied": application already happened as PR7's
   evidence, so that wording is satisfied by work predating this pass and would let W8
   be skipped — while W1–W7 change production behaviour and can revive a
   previously-killed mutation. The criterion is the re-measurement, not the backlog.
4. The standards document is on master with its gates green, and the layer passes
   them without exemptions.
5. `DESIGN.md` carries the compressed edits, each citing its decision. The charter's
   three classes remain the routes by which the layer changes outside a pass, and this
   pass retires none of them: Class A on its per-instance disclosure ceremony without
   pre-approval, Class B only on per-instance owner approval before landing, Class C
   only on an owner decision record. Class A is the only *self-serve* route, not the
   only route.
